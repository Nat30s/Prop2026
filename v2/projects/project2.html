About this project

Godrej Aristocrat brings the trusted Godrej legacy to New Chandigarh with a landmark residential development set against the Shivalik backdrop. Offering 2, 3, and 4 BHK residences across two iconic towers, each unit is designed with cross-ventilation, large balconies, and Italian marble finishes. A 2-acre central greens, Olympic-size pool, and a 30,000 sq. ft. clubhouse complete the experience.
